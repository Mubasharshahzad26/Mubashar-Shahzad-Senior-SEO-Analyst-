The Challenge: Zero AI Search Presence & Unstructured Service Pages

A local HVAC contractor in Simi Valley had no map pack presence, weak 'near me' rankings, and zero visibility in Google's new AI Overview summaries for high-ticket AC replacements.

  • No Site Structure: Services and target cities lacked dedicated landing pages, diluting geographic relevance.
  • Thin Educational Content: Lacked seasonal FAQ guides that searchers read before requesting a quote.
  • Missing LocalBusiness Schema: Search engines could not associate the brand with free in-home cost estimations.

The Strategy: Answer Engine Optimization & City Service Silos

We cleaned up technical friction, rebuilt core service pages, and engineered content to explicitly answer the questions Google's AI Overview surfaces.

Phase 01

Technical Triage & Crawl Cleanup

Fixed crawl blocks and mobile page speed so Google could index all service templates cleanly.

Phase 02

Simi Valley City Service Silos

Constructed dedicated landing pages for AC repair, maintenance, and emergency installations.

Phase 03

Direct-Answer Content for AI Overviews

Formulated pricing and cost-estimate FAQ structures designed for LLM extraction and featured snippet pickup.
