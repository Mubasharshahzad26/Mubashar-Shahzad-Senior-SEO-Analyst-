The Outcome: 490 Interactions Across April & May 2025

490 Business Profile interactions (direct phone calls, customer direction requests, and website clicks) were recorded across April and May 2025, shown on the official Business Profile Performance report.

  • What the report counts: phone calls, direction requests and website clicks across the profiles, April to May 2025.
  • Consistent business details: name, address, phone number and categories standardized across every location.

The Challenge: Inconsistent Profile Data & Stagnant Engagement

A multi-location door repair company had inconsistent Google Business Profiles across operating regions — mismatched categories, no posting cadence, and low map engagement.

The Strategy: NAP Standardization & Active Review Cadence

Standardized NAP data across all locations, refreshed service listings, and implemented a proactive review collection framework.
