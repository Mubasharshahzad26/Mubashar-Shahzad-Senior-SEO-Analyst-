5+ Years Career Arc

From Writing Content to Building SEO Systems

"SEO isn't about guessing algorithm shifts. It's about engineering cleaner site architecture, answering intent faster than competitors, and removing technical friction."

I came into SEO from an English Language and Literature background (MA), starting as a junior SEO in 2020. In 2023 I joined Remit Choice as an on-page executive and became team lead in 2024, leading a team of 8 and ranking the site #1 on Google above Wise and Xoom for a free money transfer to Pakistan from the UK search.

Between 2024 and 2026 I led SEO teams at 321Groww (roofing, cleaning and door-repair brands) and Time Technologies (HVAC Services Team and the Centaurus project), and worked on OrlandoWebPros and ADSCARRY projects. In 2026 at Momento Digital Agency I became the sole SEO owner of two US WooCommerce stores, SMK Store and Michigan Outdoor Sports.

Today I combine technical SEO with AI systems. I built SEO Autopilot to fix thin product pages at catalog scale, and GEO, AEO and AI SEO are a core part of my work, helping brands show up in AI Overviews and AI answers, not only in the blue links.

Flagship Project

SEO Autopilot: Indexing Recovery for a US WooCommerce Store

After a de-indexing event, thousands of thin brand and product pages at Michigan Outdoor Sports were crawled but not indexed, wasting crawl budget and suppressing clicks and sales. I built SEO Autopilot, a pipeline that fixes thin pages across a whole catalog. Indexed pages grew from about 4,000 to 12.2K (21 August 2026), and US clicks rose from 224 to 322 with CTR from 3.7% to 5.1% (April to August 2026). It also produced unique product copy at scale for SMK Store. Autopilot AI Visibility and Autopilot Backlinks extend the same approach.

Checks every product first and skips empty pages
Unique, product-specific AI copy, scored before it goes live
Publishes to WordPress/WooCommerce under a daily URL cap
Scheduled job queue with run logs and retries

Execution Pipeline

Operational
1
Check Each Product

Skips pages with no photo, no price or nothing to buy, so Google is never shown empty pages.

2
Write and Score

Creates unique, product-specific copy with AI (Gemini API) and scores it before anything goes live.

3
Publish to WordPress

Approved pages are published to WordPress/WooCommerce.

4
Submit and Track

Each URL is submitted for indexing under a daily cap, with run logs so every page status can be tracked and retried.
