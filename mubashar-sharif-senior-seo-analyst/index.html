5+ Years Career Arc

From In-House Corridors to Engineering Scale

"SEO isn't about guessing algorithm shifts. It's about engineering cleaner site architecture, answering intent faster than competitors, and removing technical friction."

My SEO journey began in the high-stakes world of international fintech at Remit Choice and Time Technologies. Managing high-velocity currency corridors where million-dollar transactions occur daily taught me that topical precision and zero-waste crawling beat sheer domain authority every time. Here, our team pushed corridor pages to #1 organic rankings above multi-billion-dollar competitors like Wise and Xoom, capturing over 113,000 verified clicks.

Transitioning into agency leadership as SEO Manager at Momento Digital Agency and collaborating remotely with US teams like OrlandoWebPros, I oversaw multi-disciplinary teams managing 15+ concurrent US and global accounts. From hyper-competitive home services across Texas and Michigan to massive multi-location Google Business Profiles, I implemented standardized geo-silo blueprints that delivered consistent top-3 map pack and organic rankings.

Today, I specialize in combining technical architecture with programmatic AI systems. By designing custom software like the SearchPrex SEO Autopilot, I help e-commerce stores revive suppressed catalogs and scale organic revenues without reliance on rising paid advertising costs.
