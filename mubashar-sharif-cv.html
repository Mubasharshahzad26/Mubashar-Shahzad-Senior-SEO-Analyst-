Enterprise Flagship Project

SEO Autopilot, Indexing Recovery for a US WooCommerce Store

Client

Michigan Outdoor Sports (Michigan, USA) · May to September 2026

Problem

Thousands of thin brand and product pages were crawled but not indexed after a severe de-indexing event, wasting crawl budget and suppressing clicks and sales across the entire digital catalog.

What I built

SEO Autopilot, an automated pipeline that fixes thin pages across the whole catalog:

  • Checks every product first and skips pages with no photo, no price or nothing to buy, so Google is never shown empty or unpurchasable pages.
  • Writes unique, product-specific copy with AI (Gemini API) and scores it before anything goes live.
  • Publishes approved pages to WordPress/WooCommerce and submits each URL for indexing under a daily publishing cap, so output stays steady and controlled.
  • Runs on a scheduled job queue with comprehensive run logs, so every page’s status can be tracked and retried seamlessly.
  • Stack: Next.js, Gemini API, PostgreSQL, WordPress, Google Search Console API.
Alongside the system

Submitted clean sitemaps, removed indexing blocks, optimized canonical tag hierarchy, and eliminated crawl waste.

Results

Indexed pages grew from about 4,000 to 12.2K (by 21 August 2026); US clicks rose from 224 to 322 and CTR from 3.7% to 5.1%; net sales went from $0.00 on 20 July to $523.49 month to date on 25 September 2026.
