Introduced Autopilot System

Flagship Project

SEO Autopilot, Indexing Recovery for a US WooCommerce Store

Client

Michigan Outdoor Sports (Michigan, USA) · May to September 2026

Problem

Thousands of thin brand and product pages were crawled but not indexed after a de-indexing event, wasting crawl budget and suppressing clicks and sales.

What I built

SEO Autopilot, a pipeline that fixes thin pages across the whole catalog:

  • Checks every product first and skips pages with no photo, no price or nothing to buy, so Google is never shown empty pages.
  • Writes unique, product-specific copy with AI (Gemini API) and scores it before anything goes live.
  • Publishes approved pages to WordPress/WooCommerce under a daily publishing cap, so output stays steady and controlled.
  • Runs on a scheduled job queue with run logs, so every page’s status can be tracked and retried.
  • Stack: Next.js, Gemini API, PostgreSQL, WordPress, Google Search Console.
Alongside the system

submitted sitemaps, removed indexing blocks and crawl waste.

Results

indexed pages grew from about 4,000 to 12.2K (21 August 2026); US clicks rose from 224 to 322 and CTR from 3.7% to 5.1%; net sales went from $0.00 on 20 July to $523.49 month to date on 25 September 2026.
